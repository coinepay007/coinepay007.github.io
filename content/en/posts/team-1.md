# How to Add Members to Claude Team: Setup & Permissions Guide

Claude Team is designed for businesses and organizations that want to use Claude AI collaboratively. It provides centralized member management, role-based permissions, and team billing controls.

Whether you are setting up a new workspace or managing an existing team, knowing how to invite members and configure their permissions can help keep your organization organized and secure.

This guide explains how to add members, send invitations, manage team roles, share invitation links, remove users, and troubleshoot common problems.

## 1. What You Need Before Inviting Members to Claude Team

Before adding new members, make sure your organization is properly configured.

### 1.1 Set Up a Claude Team Organization

First, you need an active Claude Team organization.

Visit the official Claude website:

[https://claude.ai/](https://claude.ai/)

Sign in with your account and make sure you are accessing the correct Team workspace.

If you have only a personal Claude account, you may need to create or upgrade to a Team organization before inviting colleagues.

### 1.2 Prepare Team Members' Email Addresses

Claude Team uses organization-approved email domains to control who can join the workspace.

Before sending invitations:

- Collect the email addresses of your team members.
- Check that each address is spelled correctly.
- Make sure the email domain is permitted by your organization.
- Confirm that members can access their inboxes.

If an email domain is not allowed, the invitation or joining process may fail.

### 1.3 Check Available Seats

Claude Team uses paid seats to provide access to team members.

Before inviting new users, check how many seats are available in your organization.

One important detail is that pending invitations occupy available seats immediately. A person does not need to accept the invitation before the seat is reserved.

If there are not enough seats, an Owner or Primary Owner may need to purchase additional seats.

## 2. How to Invite Members to Claude Team

The standard way to add members is through the organization settings.

### 2.1 Sign In to Claude Team

Open:

[https://claude.ai/](https://claude.ai/)

Sign in using an account with Admin, Owner, or Primary Owner permissions.

Make sure you are inside the correct organization rather than your personal Claude account.

### 2.2 Open the Members Management Page

Navigate to:

**Organization settings → Members**

This page allows authorized administrators to view existing members, check their roles, manage invitations, and add new users.

### 2.3 Click Add Member

On the Members page, select:

**Add member**

A window will appear where you can enter the information for the new team member.

Complete the following steps:

1. Enter the member's email address.
2. Select the appropriate seat type.
3. Choose the member's role.
4. Review the information.
5. Click **Add members** to send the invitation.

The system will send an invitation email to the address you entered.

### 2.4 Ask the Member to Accept the Invitation

After receiving the invitation, the new member needs to open the email and follow the instructions to join the organization.

According to Anthropic's current help documentation, email invitations expire after 21 days.

If the recipient does not accept the invitation within that period, an administrator will need to send another invitation.

## 3. How to Invite Multiple Claude Team Members at Once

If your company needs to add several employees, Claude Team provides a bulk invitation option.

This is particularly useful when setting up a new team or onboarding multiple employees.

### 3.1 Use the Bulk Add Option

Go to:

**Organization settings → Members → Add member**

Look for the **Bulk add** option.

### 3.2 Enter Multiple Email Addresses

You can paste multiple email addresses into the invitation field.

Separate addresses using commas or line breaks.

For example:

```
user1@company.com
user2@company.com
user3@company.com
user4@company.com
```

Check that all addresses are correct and comply with your organization's email domain requirements.

### 3.3 Submit the Invitations

After entering the email addresses:

1. Review the list of members.
2. Select the appropriate seat types.
3. Configure the roles and permissions.
4. Submit the invitations.

After submission, return to the Members page to check the invitation status.

If someone does not receive an invitation, verify their email address and check the Pending section.

## 4. How to Share a Claude Team Invitation Link

Besides sending individual email invitations, administrators can also share an invitation link with colleagues.

This method can make onboarding easier when multiple people need to join the same organization.

### 4.1 Find the Invitation Link

Sign in to Claude Team and navigate to:

**Organization settings → Identity and access**

Find the **Invite link** section under Global access settings.

Click the copy button next to the invitation link.

The availability of this feature depends on your organization type and security configuration. For example, invitation links are enabled by default for new Team organizations, while Enterprise organizations using SSO cannot use this method.

### 4.2 Share the Link with Team Members

You can distribute the invitation link through:

- Company email
- Slack channels
- Internal communication tools
- Team collaboration platforms
- Company knowledge bases

Members can open the link, sign in or create an account, and follow the joining instructions.

### 4.3 Manage Invitation Link Security

Invitation links should only be shared with authorized colleagues.

Keep the following points in mind:

- Do not publish the link on public websites.
- Share it only through trusted internal channels.
- Check whether the link is still active.
- Disable the link if it is no longer needed.
- Generate a new link if the previous one may have been exposed.

According to Anthropic's current documentation, invitation links automatically expire 90 days after creation.

Disabling or regenerating a link immediately invalidates the existing link.

Members joining through an invitation link are assigned to the lowest available seat tier. If no seats are available, the joining process may require additional seat allocation.

## 5. Claude Team Roles and Permissions Explained

After inviting members, you should assign appropriate roles according to their responsibilities.

Claude Team provides four main roles:

- User
- Admin
- Owner
- Primary Owner

Each role has different permissions.

### 5.1 User: Standard Team Member

The User role is intended for employees who primarily use Claude for everyday work.

Typical permissions include:

- Creating and modifying chats
- Using Projects
- Accessing available team features
- Working with shared resources according to organization settings

Users generally cannot manage organization members or billing settings.

### 5.2 Admin: Team Administrator

Admins are responsible for day-to-day organization management.

Their permissions include:

- Inviting new members
- Removing members
- Canceling pending invitations
- Managing member access within their assigned permissions

However, Admins cannot perform every organization-level action.

For example, they cannot access billing settings or change member roles in the same way as Owners.

### 5.3 Owner: Organization Owner

Owners have broader management permissions.

Depending on the organization's configuration, they can:

- Invite and remove members
- Manage Admin and Owner assignments
- Modify member roles
- View and pay invoices
- Add or modify payment methods
- Purchase additional seats

Owners play an important role in managing the organization's subscription and membership.

### 5.4 Primary Owner: Main Organization Owner

The Primary Owner is the main owner of the Claude Team organization.

This role has extensive administrative and billing permissions.

The Primary Owner can manage important organization settings, oversee seat allocation, and transfer Primary Owner responsibilities to another eligible member.

Each organization can have only one Primary Owner at a time.

For security reasons, this role should be assigned to someone responsible for the organization's overall account management.

## 6. How to Change a Claude Team Member's Role

If an employee's responsibilities change, you may need to update their permissions.

Only Owners and Primary Owners can modify member roles.

Follow these steps:

1. Sign in to your Claude Team organization.
2. Open **Organization settings**.
3. Select **Members**.
4. Find the member whose role needs to change.
5. Open the role dropdown next to their name.
6. Select the appropriate role.
7. Confirm the change.

For example, if an employee becomes responsible for onboarding new team members, an Owner may upgrade their role from User to Admin.

If you cannot find the role modification option, check whether your account has sufficient permissions.

## 7. How to Remove a Member from Claude Team

When an employee leaves the company or no longer needs access, you can remove them from the organization.

### 7.1 Open the Members Page

Sign in to Claude Team and navigate to:

**Organization settings → Members**

Find the member you want to remove.

### 7.2 Remove the Member

Click the menu button next to the member's name.

Select:

**Remove from team**

Follow the confirmation instructions to complete the process.

### 7.3 What Happens After Removing a Member?

When a member is removed:

- Their access to the organization is revoked immediately.
- Their assigned seat becomes available for another member.
- The seat can be reassigned to someone else.
- If the same person is added again using the same email address, their account history is maintained.

Keep in mind that removing a member does not automatically reduce the total number of seats purchased by your organization.

If you want to reduce your subscription's seat allocation, you must manage the seat count separately.

An Owner or Primary Owner generally cannot remove themselves from the organization. Another eligible owner must handle that action.

## 8. Common Claude Team Invitation Problems and Solutions

Sometimes invitations fail or members cannot join the organization. Here are some common issues and ways to address them.

### 8.1 Email Domain Not Allowed

One common issue is an email address that does not match the organization's permitted domains.

Possible solutions:

- Check the spelling of the email address.
- Confirm that the domain is permitted.
- Ask an administrator to review the organization's allowed email domains.
- Use an eligible business email address if required.

### 8.2 Not Enough Available Seats

If all available seats are assigned or reserved by pending invitations, you may not be able to add another member.

Try the following:

- Review the current seat allocation.
- Check whether any members no longer need access.
- Remove unnecessary members to free up seats.
- Revoke invitations that will not be accepted.
- Ask an Owner or Primary Owner to purchase additional seats if necessary.

### 8.3 Invitation Email Not Received

If a team member cannot find the invitation email, check the following:

- Is the email address correct?
- Has the invitation been sent successfully?
- Is the message in the spam or junk folder?
- Is the invitation still pending?
- Has the invitation expired?

To resend an expired invitation:

1. Open **Organization settings → Members**.
2. Select the **Pending** tab.
3. Find the relevant email address.
4. Choose the option to resend the invitation.

### 8.4 Unable to Change Member Permissions

If you cannot modify a member's role, your current account may not have sufficient permissions.

According to Anthropic's role and permission documentation, only Owners and Primary Owners can modify member roles.

Contact an Owner if you need additional administrative access.

### 8.5 Invitation Link Does Not Work

An invitation link may stop working if it has expired, been disabled, or been regenerated.

Ask an administrator to check the link under:

**Organization settings → Identity and access**

If necessary, the administrator can generate a new link and share it with the team.

## 9. Best Practices for Managing a Claude Team Workspace

For companies and organizations with multiple users, good membership management helps maintain security and control subscription costs.

### 9.1 Assign Roles Based on Job Responsibilities

Avoid giving every employee Admin or Owner permissions.

A practical approach is:

- User: Regular employees
- Admin: Staff responsible for team management
- Owner: Organization and subscription managers
- Primary Owner: Main organization account administrator

Giving members only the permissions they need helps reduce unnecessary access.

### 9.2 Review the Member List Regularly

Check your Members page periodically.

Pay attention to:

- Former employees
- Members who no longer use Claude
- Invitations that have not been accepted
- Accounts with unnecessary administrative permissions

Removing unused accounts can also free up seats for new employees.

### 9.3 Plan Seat Allocation Carefully

Before inviting additional users, review the number and types of available seats.

Claude Team offers different seat types, including Standard and Premium. Their features and usage limits differ.

Assign seat types according to each employee's actual workload instead of automatically choosing the same option for everyone.

### 9.4 Protect Invitation Links

Invitation links can provide direct access to your organization.

Only share them with trusted colleagues. If a link is accidentally exposed, disable it or generate a replacement.

For organizations that need tighter access control, individual email invitations may be preferable because administrators can review invitations before members join.

## 11. Conclusion

Inviting members and managing permissions are important parts of operating a Claude Team organization.

Administrators can add new members, manage pending invitations, and remove users through the Members page. Owners and Primary Owners have additional permissions for role management, billing, and seat allocation.

For teams that need to onboard multiple employees, the Bulk add feature and invitation links can make the process more convenient.

To keep your organization secure and organized, remember to:

- Assign roles according to responsibilities.
- Check available seats before sending invitations.
- Review pending invitations regularly.
- Remove accounts that are no longer needed.
- Protect invitation links.
- Monitor subscription costs and billing settings.

By following these practices, your organization can manage Claude Team members more efficiently and maintain better control over workspace access.
