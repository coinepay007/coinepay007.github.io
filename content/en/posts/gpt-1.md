## 1. What Is ChatGPT Ads?

ChatGPT Ads is OpenAI's advertising solution for businesses that want to reach audiences through the ChatGPT experience.

Advertisers can use OpenAI Ads Manager to manage their advertising activities, including:

- Creating and managing advertising campaigns
- Setting campaign objectives and budgets
- Organizing advertisements into ad groups
- Uploading advertising images and writing ad copy

OpenAI Ads Manager is currently a beta product, so its features, advertising formats, and availability may change over time.

## 2. Requirements for Opening a ChatGPT Ads Account

Before registering, prepare the following information.

### 2.1 An OpenAI Account

You need an OpenAI account to access Ads Manager. You can sign in with an existing account or create a new one.

For business advertising, using an account associated with your work email can make account management easier, especially when multiple team members need access.

### 2.2 Business and Advertiser Information

Prepare your business information before starting registration. Depending on the account setup and verification requirements, you may need:

- Advertiser or business name
- Legal business name
- Business website
- Business address
- Brand logo
- Contact information

Make sure the information is accurate and consistent with your business documents.

### 2.3 A Supported Country or Region

Self-service access to Ads Manager depends on the country or region of the legal entity responsible for advertising and billing.

OpenAI maintains an official availability list. The supported countries and regions may change as the service expands.

Check the latest availability before preparing your advertising account:

[https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001245-ads-manager-availability](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001245-ads-manager-availability)

If self-service access is not yet available in your country, you may still be able to create an account and receive a notification when access becomes available. However, account creation does not mean you can start running advertisements immediately.

### 2.4 A Supported Payment Method

For self-service card-billed accounts, you need to create a billing profile and add an eligible payment card.

Prepare your business billing information, including:

- Business name
- Billing address
- Invoice email address
- Credit card information

Payment acceptance may depend on the country associated with your advertiser account and the card's billing details.

## 3. How to Register for a ChatGPT Ads Account

Follow these steps to create your advertiser account.

### Step 1: Open the Official Ads Manager Website

Visit the official OpenAI Ads Manager website:

[https://ads.openai.com/](https://ads.openai.com/)

Select **Sign Up** if you do not have an OpenAI account, or choose **Login** if you already have one.

Use your OpenAI account to access the advertising platform.

### Step 2: Choose Your Registration Method

During registration, you may be offered two ways to get started.

**Option A: Start with Your Website**

Enter your business website URL when prompted. Ads Manager can use this information to help you begin creating your first advertisement and campaign during the onboarding process.

**Option B: Skip Campaign Creation**

You can also skip the initial advertisement creation step and go directly into Ads Manager.

This option is useful if you want to complete your account settings first and prepare your campaign later.

Both methods allow you to create an advertiser account.

### Step 3: Enter Your Advertiser Information

Follow the instructions to enter your advertiser name and other required account details.

During the initial setup, information may be used to populate fields such as:

- Account Name
- Brand Name
- Legal Name

Review these details carefully before continuing.

Some information can be updated later in account settings, but changing brand or legal information may trigger additional reviews.

### Step 4: Select Your Country, Currency, and Time Zone

This is one of the most important steps in the registration process.

Choose the correct:

- Country or region
- Advertising account currency
- Account time zone

According to OpenAI's setup guidance, the account's country or region, currency, and time zone cannot be changed after the account is created.

Check these settings carefully before confirming your registration.

### Step 5: Complete Account Setup

After entering the required information, continue through the remaining onboarding steps.

Depending on your account, you may need to add a brand logo, complete account verification, and configure your billing profile.

Once the required setup is complete, you can access the Ads Manager dashboard and prepare your advertising campaigns.

## 4. How to Set Up ChatGPT Ads Billing and Payments

A completed billing setup is required before campaigns can begin delivering.

For self-service card-billed accounts, the process generally involves creating a billing profile and adding a payment method.

### Step 1: Create a Billing Profile

Open the billing section in Ads Manager and enter your business billing information.

You may need to provide:

- Business name
- Business address
- Invoice delivery email

Use accurate information so that billing records and invoices can be managed properly.

### Step 2: Add a Payment Card

Enter your credit card details and billing address.

Make sure the payment information is consistent with the advertiser account's country and business details.

A card issued in another country may not be accepted.

### Step 3: Understand the Billing Model

ChatGPT Ads currently uses a postpay billing model for self-service card-billed accounts.

This means advertising costs accumulate as your campaigns deliver, and the saved payment method is charged according to the account's assigned payment threshold and billing arrangements.

OpenAI may also place a temporary authorization hold when verifying a payment card. This is not necessarily a completed advertising charge. The release time can depend on the bank.

### Step 4: Set Your Advertising Budget

When creating a campaign, you can configure its budget.

The available budget types include:

**Daily Budget**

A daily budget represents the average amount you want the campaign to spend per day over a seven-day period. It is not necessarily a strict spending limit for each individual day.

**Campaign Total Budget**

A campaign total budget sets the maximum amount you are willing to spend across the entire campaign.

Review the budget carefully before publishing. Actual spending depends on campaign delivery and the pricing model you select.

## 5. How to Create Your First ChatGPT Ads Campaign

After completing account registration and billing setup, you can begin creating your first campaign.

### Step 1: Create a Campaign

Open Ads Manager and select the option to create a campaign.

Choose an advertising objective based on what you want to achieve, such as:

- Increasing brand visibility
- Driving website traffic
- Encouraging conversions

Configure the campaign budget and other available settings.

### Step 2: Create an Ad Group

An ad group organizes advertisements around a particular theme or intent area.

You may be able to add context hints to describe the types of conversations relevant to your advertising group.

Use clear and relevant information to help organize your campaign structure.

### Step 3: Create Your Advertisement

Prepare the advertising materials, which may include:

- Ad title
- Advertising copy
- Image or brand creative
- Landing page URL

Your advertising content should accurately describe your product or service. Avoid misleading claims, unclear pricing, or unsupported promises.

### Step 4: Submit Your Campaign for Review

Before an advertisement can appear, it must pass the applicable review process.

Check the campaign and advertisement status in Ads Manager. If an advertisement is rejected or marked as not serving, review the displayed reason and make any necessary changes.

An approved advertisement can begin delivering only when the account, billing, and campaign requirements are also satisfied.

## 6. How to Monitor ChatGPT Ads Performance

After your campaign starts running, use Ads Manager to monitor its performance.

Common advertising metrics include:

**Impressions:** The number of times your advertisement is displayed.

**Clicks:** The number of times users click your advertisement.

**CTR:** The click-through rate, which measures clicks relative to impressions.

**Average CPC:** The average cost per click.

**Average CPM:** The average cost per thousand impressions.

**Spend:** The advertising costs accumulated during the selected period.

**Conversions:** Actions measured through the configured conversion measurement system.

Review these metrics regularly to understand how your campaigns are performing.

You can use the results to improve advertising copy, adjust budgets, refine campaign structures, and test different landing pages.

Keep in mind that some reporting data may not update immediately. In particular, spending information can be delayed.

## 7. Common ChatGPT Ads Registration and Payment Problems

New advertisers may encounter several issues during account setup.

### Problem 1: Ads Manager Is Not Available in My Country

Self-service advertising access is not available everywhere.

Check the official country availability list. If your country is not supported, you may need to wait for OpenAI to expand access or follow the onboarding instructions provided by an authorized OpenAI advertising partner.

### Problem 2: My Payment Card Is Declined

A card may be declined because of regional restrictions, incorrect billing information, bank security controls, or unsupported payment methods.

Check the following:

- Whether the card supports the required type of online transaction
- Whether the billing address is correct
- Whether the card issuer allows the transaction
- Whether the card's country is consistent with the advertiser account

If the issue continues, contact your card issuer or OpenAI Ads support.

### Problem 3: My Account Was Created but I Cannot Run Ads

Creating an advertiser account is only the first step.

You may still need to:

- Complete business verification
- Finish billing setup
- Resolve outstanding payment issues
- Submit advertisements for review
- Correct campaign settings

Check the account and campaign status messages in Ads Manager for the specific reason.

### Problem 4: My Account Country or Currency Is Incorrect

The country or region, currency, and time zone cannot be changed after an advertiser account is created.

Review these settings carefully before completing registration. If you need another account with different settings, check the official account setup guidance to see which options are available for your organization.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions About ChatGPT Ads Accounts

### Can Individuals Create a ChatGPT Ads Account?

Account access depends on the current registration requirements, supported country or region, and advertiser verification process. Check the information requested during registration to determine whether your account type is eligible.

No. Self-service access depends on the country or region of the legal entity responsible for advertising and billing. OpenAI may expand availability over time.

### Do I Need a Website to Register?

You may be able to skip the website-based onboarding flow and enter Ads Manager directly. However, you should prepare a business website or landing page if your advertising campaign requires one.

### Can I Change My Account Currency After Registration?

Your advertisements must pass review, and your account, billing profile, and campaign settings must be ready. Account creation alone does not start advertising.
