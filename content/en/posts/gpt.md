If your subscription payment fails, check the following common causes before trying again.

Avoid repeatedly submitting failed payments without identifying the problem.

### ChatGPT Plus Is Not Available at Checkout

If you cannot find the Plus subscription option:

1. Confirm that you are signed in to the correct account.
2. Check the official ChatGPT plan page.
3. Verify that your location is supported.
4. Refresh the official website or update the app.
5. Contact OpenAI Support if the problem continues.

Avoid third-party services that guarantee subscription access without explaining their payment and account arrangements.

### You Were Charged but Still Have the Free Plan

First, verify that you are signed in to the same ChatGPT account used for the purchase.

If you subscribed through Apple or Google Play, check the subscription status in the corresponding store account.

If the payment has been completed but Plus is still unavailable, contact official support and provide the relevant transaction information. Never send your full card number or verification codes to an unknown person.

### You See Duplicate Charges

Duplicate charges can occur if you have active subscriptions through different platforms or accounts.

Check your billing records on ChatGPT, Apple, and Google Play, as applicable. Identify which platform processed each payment and cancel any unwanted subscription through the platform that manages it.

## 7. How to Manage or Cancel Your ChatGPT Plus Subscription

After subscribing, it is useful to understand billing, renewal, and cancellation.

### Check Your Subscription

If you subscribed on the web:

1. Sign in to ChatGPT.
2. Open your account menu.
3. Go to Settings.
4. Open Billing or the relevant subscription management section.
5. Review your plan and billing information.

If you subscribed through Apple or Google Play, check the subscription settings in the store account used for the purchase.

### Understand Automatic Renewal

Paid subscriptions generally renew according to their billing terms unless canceled.

Before purchasing, review the renewal date and conditions so you know when the next payment may occur.

### Cancel Your Subscription

The cancellation process depends on where you purchased the subscription.

**For web subscriptions:**

1. Sign in to ChatGPT.
2. Open your account menu and select Settings.
3. Open Billing.
4. Find the cancellation option and follow the instructions.

**For Apple App Store subscriptions:**

1. Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Tap your name.
3. Select Subscriptions.
4. Choose ChatGPT.
5. Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm.

**For Google Play subscriptions:**

1. Open Google Play.
2. Go to Subscriptions.
3. Select ChatGPT.
4. Choose Cancel subscription.
5. Follow the confirmation instructions.

Canceling generally stops future renewals; it does not automatically refund previous charges. Uninstalling the app also does not cancel a subscription.

For the latest steps, visit OpenAI's [official cancellation guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7232927-canceling-your-chatgpt-subscription).
