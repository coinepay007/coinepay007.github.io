## 1. Common Reasons Why TikTok Ads Get Rejected

TikTok reviews advertising content to ensure that ads comply with its advertising policies and applicable local regulations.

Here are some of the most common reasons why an advertisement may fail the review process.

### 1.1 Misleading or Exaggerated Advertising Claims

One of the most common reasons for ad rejection is making exaggerated, misleading, or unsupported claims.

Examples include:

- Guaranteed financial results without reliable evidence.
- Unrealistic product performance promises.
- Claims that a product can solve every problem.
- Unsupported statements such as "100% guaranteed results."
- Misleading discounts or promotional offers.

For example, an advertisement claiming that users can become rich overnight or achieve guaranteed results may raise compliance concerns.

**How to fix it:**

- Remove exaggerated statements.
- Use accurate and verifiable product descriptions.
- Avoid promising results that cannot be guaranteed.
- Make sure promotional claims can be supported by evidence.
- Clearly explain what the product or service actually offers.

Advertising copy should be informative and honest rather than relying on unrealistic promises.

### 1.2 Poor Video or Image Quality

TikTok is a video-focused platform, so the quality of advertising creatives matters.

Ads may encounter review problems when they contain:

- Blurry or low-resolution videos.
- Poorly edited images.
- Incomplete or unreadable text.
- Black bars or unnecessary visual obstructions.
- Missing or unclear audio.
- Audio that is difficult to understand.

**How to fix it:**

- Use clear, high-quality video and images.
- Check that all text is readable.
- Make sure important visual elements are not covered.
- Use clear and appropriate audio.
- Preview the complete video before submitting it.

A professional-looking advertisement can improve the user experience, but creative quality alone does not guarantee approval.

### 1.3 Incorrect Spelling and Grammar in Ad Copy

Text errors can also cause advertising review problems.

Common examples include:

- Spelling mistakes.
- Incorrect grammar.
- Excessive capitalization.
- Unusual symbols replacing normal letters.
- Incomplete sentences.
- Text covered by stickers or other visual elements.

For example, writing "S@le" instead of "Sale" to attract attention may create unnecessary review concerns.

**How to fix it:**

Review the headline, description, subtitles, and text inside the video before submitting the advertisement.

Use natural language, correct spelling, and clear descriptions.

### 1.4 Ad Content Does Not Match the Landing Page

Consistency between the advertisement and its landing page is extremely important.

For example:

- The video promotes Product A, but the website displays Product B.
- The ad promises a 50% discount, but the website only offers 20%.
- The advertisement describes a service that cannot be found on the landing page.
- The ad promotes a specific product, but the website contains unrelated information.

These inconsistencies can create a misleading experience for users.

**How to fix it:**

- Make sure the advertised product is clearly displayed on the website.
- Keep prices and discounts consistent.
- Ensure the landing page explains the service shown in the ad.
- Avoid using unrelated images or generic content.
- Update expired promotional information.

The advertisement and landing page should provide a consistent and accurate description of the same offer.

### 1.5 Landing Page Does Not Work Properly

A landing page that fails to load or function correctly can lead to ad rejection.

Common problems include:

- A 404 error page.
- An expired website.
- A page that is still under construction.
- Broken links or incorrect redirects.
- Slow loading times.
- Poor mobile compatibility.
- Missing essential website information.

**How to fix it:**

Before submitting your advertisement:

1. Open the landing page on a desktop browser.
2. Test it on a mobile phone.
3. Check whether all links work.
4. Confirm that the page loads correctly.
5. Remove incomplete or broken sections.
6. Make sure visitors can access the advertised product or service.

A functional and user-friendly landing page is an important part of a compliant advertising campaign.

### 1.6 Missing Website Information

Depending on the business type and target market, a website may need to display certain information.

Examples include:

- Business or company information.
- Contact details.
- Privacy policy.
- Terms and conditions.
- Refund and return policies for relevant businesses.
- Accurate product pricing and service descriptions.

For e-commerce and certain regulated industries, additional information may be required by local laws and TikTok's advertising policies.

**How to fix it:**

Review your website and add the information required for your business and target market.

Make sure important policies are easy to find and written clearly.

### 1.7 Unauthorized Use of Third-Party Logos or Copyrighted Materials

Using another company's logo, copyrighted video, music, images, or trademarks without permission can create intellectual property issues.

Examples include:

- Using a brand logo without authorization.
- Copying another advertiser's video.
- Using copyrighted images without permission.
- Making unsupported claims of official brand partnerships.
- Using TikTok branding in a way that suggests unauthorized endorsement.

**How to fix it:**

- Use original advertising materials whenever possible.
- Obtain permission before using third-party brand assets.
- Use properly licensed images, videos, and music.
- Keep authorization documents when applicable.
- Avoid suggesting partnerships that do not exist.

### 1.8 Restricted or Prohibited Products and Services

TikTok has different advertising requirements for different industries.

Some products and services are prohibited, while others may be subject to additional restrictions, documentation, or market-specific requirements.

Requirements can vary depending on:

- The advertised product or service.
- The target country or region.
- The intended audience.
- Applicable local regulations.
- Required business licenses or certifications.

**How to fix it:**

Check TikTok's industry-specific advertising policies before launching your campaign.

If your industry requires additional documentation, prepare the relevant information before submitting your ads.

Do not attempt to bypass restrictions by hiding the actual nature of a product or service.

## 2. How to Check Why Your TikTok Ad Was Rejected

If your advertisement is rejected, the first step is to identify the exact reason.

Follow these steps:

### Step 1: Log in to TikTok Ads Manager

Visit the official TikTok Ads Manager website and log in using the account associated with your campaign.

### Step 2: Open the Campaign Section

Navigate to the Campaign page and locate the rejected advertisement or ad group.

### Step 3: Check the Ad Status

Look for the rejection status displayed beside the relevant campaign, ad group, or ad.

Open the available details to review the rejection notification.

### Step 4: Read the Rejection Reason

Pay attention to the specific policy or content issue identified by TikTok.

For example, the problem may involve:

- Ad creative content.
- Promotional claims.
- Landing page functionality.
- Industry restrictions.
- Intellectual property.
- Target market requirements.

### Step 5: Decide Whether to Edit or Appeal

If the advertisement contains an actual policy issue, correct it before submitting again.

If you believe the ad was incorrectly rejected and it complies with the applicable policies, check whether an appeal or review option is available.

## 3. How to Fix a Rejected TikTok Ad

Once you identify the problem, follow a systematic correction process.

### Fix 1: Rewrite the Advertising Copy

Remove exaggerated claims, misleading statements, and unsupported promises.

Use clear language to describe the product's real features and benefits.

### Fix 2: Improve the Video Creative

Review the video from beginning to end.

Check the resolution, subtitles, audio, editing, and visual presentation.

Replace any elements that may violate advertising requirements.

### Fix 3: Update the Landing Page

Make sure the destination website works correctly and displays the product or service advertised.

Check prices, product descriptions, promotional information, and important website policies.

### Fix 4: Review Industry Requirements

If your product belongs to a regulated or restricted industry, confirm whether advertising is permitted in your target market.

Prepare any required documentation.

### Fix 5: Submit the Ad for Another Review

After making the necessary changes, save the updated advertisement.

Depending on the available workflow, editing and saving the ad may automatically trigger another review.

Follow the status shown in TikTok Ads Manager.

Avoid repeatedly submitting the same unchanged advertisement.

## 4. What Should You Do If Your TikTok Ad Is Rejected by Mistake?

Sometimes advertisers believe their ads comply with TikTok's policies but still receive a rejection notification.

If you believe the decision is incorrect, you may be able to request another review.

Before submitting an appeal:

1. Read the rejection notification carefully.
2. Check the relevant advertising policy.
3. Review the complete ad creative and landing page.
4. Gather relevant supporting information.
5. Explain clearly why you believe the ad complies with the policy.

### Example TikTok Ads Appeal Message

You can use the following English template as a starting point if you genuinely believe your advertisement was incorrectly rejected.

**Subject: Request for Ad Review**

Hello TikTok Support Team,

My advertisement was rejected during the review process. I have carefully reviewed the ad copy, video creative, promoted product, and landing page to ensure they comply with TikTok's advertising policies.

I believe the rejection may have been made in error and would appreciate it if your team could review the advertisement again.

If there are any specific issues that still need to be addressed, please provide additional details so I can make the necessary corrections.

Thank you for your time and assistance.

Best regards,

Advertiser

Only use an appeal when you have a genuine reason to believe the rejection was incorrect. If the ad contains a real policy violation, correcting the issue is the more appropriate approach.

## 5. Why Does TikTok Ads Get Rejected Even After Approval?

An advertisement passing its initial review does not necessarily mean it can never be reviewed again.

TikTok may conduct additional reviews after an ad has been approved.

Possible reasons include:

- New information about the advertisement.
- Changes to advertising policy requirements.
- User reports or complaints.
- Changes to the promoted website.
- Further checks of the advertised product or service.

If an approved ad is later found to violate advertising policies, it may be suspended or stopped.

Therefore, advertisers should continue monitoring their campaigns after approval.

## 6. How to Prevent TikTok Ads From Being Rejected

Preventing problems before submission is usually more efficient than repeatedly correcting rejected advertisements.

Here is a practical checklist.

### Advertising Copy Checklist

- Use accurate product descriptions.
- Avoid unrealistic promises.
- Remove misleading promotional claims.
- Check spelling and grammar.
- Avoid excessive capitalization and unusual symbols.

### Video and Image Checklist

- Use clear and high-quality media.
- Make sure subtitles are readable.
- Check audio quality.
- Remove inappropriate visual content.
- Use authorized images, music, and brand assets.

### Landing Page Checklist

- Make sure the website loads correctly.
- Check mobile compatibility.
- Keep the landing page consistent with the advertisement.
- Display accurate product information.
- Provide relevant contact details and policies.
- Avoid broken links and incomplete pages.

### Policy Compliance Checklist

- Check industry-specific advertising requirements.
- Confirm that the product is eligible for promotion.
- Review the rules for your target market.
- Prepare required business documents.
- Avoid prohibited products and misleading content.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions About TikTok Ads Rejections

### Why does TikTok keep rejecting my ads?

Repeated rejections may indicate that the advertisement still contains a policy issue. Common problems include misleading claims, poor-quality creatives, landing page errors, inconsistent product information, or restricted products.

Review the rejection details and correct the underlying issue before submitting again.

### How long does TikTok Ads review take?

TikTok states that most ads are reviewed within approximately 24 hours, although some reviews may take longer.

You can monitor the latest status through TikTok Ads Manager.

### Can I edit a rejected TikTok ad?

Yes. If the rejection reason can be corrected, you can update the relevant content and submit the ad for another review.

### Does a rejected ad mean my advertising account is banned?

No. An individual ad rejection and an advertising account suspension are different situations.

Check whether the restriction applies to the ad, ad group, campaign, or the entire advertising account.

### Can I appeal a rejected TikTok ad?

You may be able to request a review if the available appeal process supports your case. Follow the instructions shown in TikTok Ads Manager or the official advertiser support channel.

### Can a landing page cause ad rejection?

Yes. A broken website, inconsistent product information, prohibited content, or missing required information may cause an advertisement to fail review.

### Can I use a virtual card to pay for TikTok Ads?

This depends on the payment methods supported in your region and the card issuer's restrictions. Check compatibility before adding a virtual card to your advertising account.
