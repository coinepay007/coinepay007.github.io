1. What Is X Premium?

X Premium is a paid subscription service offered by X. It provides additional features and account benefits beyond those available to standard users.

Depending on your subscription tier, region, and account eligibility, available features may include:

- Blue checkmark eligibility
- Editing published posts
- Longer posts
- Longer video uploads
- Reduced advertising in certain subscription tiers
- Reply prioritization in eligible situations
- Access to additional premium features

The exact benefits may change over time. Before subscribing, review the features displayed on the official X Premium page.

It is important to understand that paying for a subscription does not automatically guarantee a blue checkmark. Your account must still meet X's eligibility and verification requirements.

2. What Do You Need Before Subscribing?

Before purchasing X Premium, prepare the following:

You need an X account that you can access normally.

- Profile picture

- Bio
- Basic account information

Accounts with certain restrictions or unusual activity may not be eligible for subscription or verification.

A Supported Payment Method

X Premium requires a payment method supported by your region and subscription channel.

Depending on your location, available options may include credit cards, debit cards, or payments through an app store.

The available payment methods can vary, so always check the options shown during checkout.

Check the Subscription Price

X Premium may offer different subscription tiers and billing periods.

Prices can vary depending on your country, subscription tier, and whether you subscribe through the website or a mobile app.

Review the displayed price and renewal terms before completing your purchase.

3. How to Get X Premium: Step-by-Step Guide

You can subscribe through the official X website by following these steps.

Step 1: Open the X Website

Visit the official website:

[https://x.com/](https://x.com/)

Log in using your X username and password.

If you do not have an account, create one first and complete any required verification steps.

Step 2: Open the X Premium Page

After logging in, look for Premium in the navigation menu.

You can also visit the official subscription page:

[https://x.com/i/premium](https://x.com/i/premium)

The page should display the available subscription plans for your account and region.

Step 3: Choose a Subscription Plan

X Premium may offer several subscription tiers with different features and prices.

Before selecting a plan, check:

- Whether the plan includes blue checkmark eligibility
- Which premium features are included
- The monthly or annual price
- Whether automatic renewal applies
- The cancellation policy

Choose the plan that best matches your needs. Do not assume that every subscription tier includes identical benefits.

Step 4: Select a Payment Method

After choosing a plan, continue to the checkout page.

Select one of the payment methods available in your region.

If card payment is supported, you may need to provide:

- Card number
- Expiration date
- CVV or CVC security code
- Cardholder information
- Billing address, if required

Make sure all information is accurate and matches the details associated with your payment method.

Step 5: Complete the Payment

Review the subscription plan, payment amount, and billing period.

If everything is correct, submit your payment.

Your bank or card issuer may require additional verification, such as 3D Secure authentication.

Once the payment is successful, return to your X account and check your subscription status.

Step 6: Wait for Account Review

After subscribing, X may review your account to determine whether it meets the requirements for the blue checkmark.

If your account meets the applicable requirements, the blue checkmark may appear after the review process.

If it does not appear immediately, check your account notifications and subscription status for further instructions.

5. What to Do If Your X Premium Payment Fails

If your subscription payment is declined, try these troubleshooting steps.

Check Your Card Details

Make sure the card number, expiration date, CVV, and billing address are correct.

Even a small mistake can cause a payment to fail.

Check Your Available Balance

Ensure that your card has enough available funds or credit to cover the subscription charge.

Check International Payment Settings

Some banks restrict international transactions by default.

Contact your bank or card issuer to confirm whether international online payments are enabled.

Check Recurring Payment Support

X Premium is a subscription service that may involve automatic renewal.

Some cards support regular online purchases but do not support recurring payments. Ask your card issuer whether subscription transactions are allowed.

Check Your X Account Status

Account restrictions or eligibility issues may prevent you from subscribing.

Review any notifications from X and follow the instructions provided.

Avoid Repeated Payment Attempts

If a payment fails, do not keep submitting the same transaction repeatedly.

First, check whether the transaction is pending and contact your card issuer if necessary.

6. How to Cancel Your X Premium Subscription

X Premium is generally billed as a recurring subscription. If you do not want to be charged for the next billing period, you need to cancel the subscription before renewal.

If you subscribed through the X website, follow these general steps:

1. Log in to your X account.
2. Open the Premium section.
3. Find the subscription management option.
4. Look for the cancellation option.
5. Follow the instructions to confirm cancellation.

If you subscribed through Apple App Store or Google Play, you may need to cancel through the subscription settings of the relevant app store.

Canceling automatic renewal does not necessarily remove your existing benefits immediately. Your subscription may remain active until the end of the current paid period, depending on the applicable terms.
