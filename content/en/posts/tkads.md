## 1. What Is TikTok Ads?

TikTok Ads is TikTok's official advertising platform. Businesses and advertisers can use TikTok Ads Manager to create, manage, and optimize advertising campaigns.

Unlike ordinary TikTok video posts, paid advertisements allow advertisers to reach audiences based on campaign objectives, locations, demographics, interests, and other available targeting options.

TikTok Ads can be used for several purposes:

- **E-commerce promotion:** Advertise products, online stores, and independent websites.
- **Brand awareness:** Introduce a brand or product to a wider audience.
- **Website traffic:** Send TikTok users to a website or landing page.
- **App promotion:** Encourage users to download and use mobile applications.
- **Lead generation:** Collect information from potential customers.
- **Product sales:** Encourage customers to purchase products through supported sales destinations.

Before launching a campaign, identify your main business objective. A clear goal makes it easier to choose the right campaign settings and evaluate advertising performance.

## 4. Understanding the TikTok Ads Campaign Structure

Before launching an advertisement, it is important to understand how TikTok Ads Manager organizes campaigns.

The platform uses three main levels:

### 4.1 Campaign

A Campaign is the highest level of an advertising structure.

At this level, you select the advertising objective and configure campaign-level settings, including the campaign name and available budget options.

Examples of campaign goals include:

- Increasing website traffic.
- Generating leads.
- Increasing video views.
- Promoting products and sales.
- Building brand awareness.

### 4.2 Ad Group

An Ad Group controls how your advertisements are delivered.

Common settings include:

- Target locations.
- Audience targeting.
- Available demographic options.
- Interests and behaviors.
- Ad placements.
- Advertising schedule.
- Budget.
- Bidding and optimization settings.

One Campaign can contain multiple Ad Groups, allowing you to test different audiences and advertising strategies.

### 4.3 Ad

An Ad is the actual advertisement that users see on TikTok.

It typically includes:

- Video or image creative.
- Advertising text.
- TikTok account identity.
- Call-to-action button.
- Destination URL or another supported destination.

One Ad Group can contain multiple ads, making it easier to compare different videos, images, and messages.

In simple terms:

- **Campaign:** Determines your advertising objective.
- **Ad Group:** Controls targeting, placement, budget, and delivery settings.
- **Ad:** Contains the creative content users see.

## 5. How to Run TikTok Ads: Step-by-Step Tutorial

Now that you understand the campaign structure, you can create your first advertisement.

### Step 1: Open the Campaign Creation Page

Log in to TikTok Ads Manager.

Go to the Campaign section or Dashboard and click **Create** or **Create ad**, depending on the interface available to your account.

Follow the campaign creation process displayed on the screen.

Depending on your account and campaign setup, you may see options for manual campaign creation and automated advertising features such as Smart+.

If you are a beginner and want to understand each setting, start by learning the manual campaign creation process.

### Step 2: Choose an Advertising Objective

Your advertising objective tells TikTok what result you want to achieve.

TikTok Ads organizes objectives around three main categories.

**1. Awareness**

This category is suitable for advertisers who want to introduce their brand or products to more people.

Common uses include:

- Brand promotion.
- Product exposure.
- Increasing brand recognition.
- Reaching a broader audience.

**2. Consideration**

This category is suitable when you want users to learn more about your business or interact with your content.

Depending on the options available in your account, objectives may include:

- Website traffic.
- Video views.
- Community interaction.
- Other engagement-related goals.

**3. Conversion**

This category focuses on encouraging users to complete specific actions.

Examples include:

- Purchasing products.
- Registering for a service.
- Submitting a lead form.
- Completing actions in a mobile application.

Choose an objective based on your actual business goal rather than simply selecting the most popular option.

For example, if you want users to visit your website, consider a traffic objective. If you want customers to purchase products, choose an available sales or conversion objective that matches your tracking setup.

### Step 3: Set Your Campaign Name and Budget

After choosing an objective, enter a campaign name.

Use a naming format that makes your campaigns easy to identify.

For example:

- US_Website_Traffic_2027
- UK_Product_Sales_2027
- JP_Brand_Awareness_2027

If campaign-level budget settings are available, choose the budget strategy that matches your advertising plan.

Common budget options include:

- **Daily Budget:** Sets the planned amount to spend per day.
- **Lifetime Budget:** Sets the total planned budget for the campaign period.

If this is your first campaign, start with a budget you can comfortably afford to test. Review the results before increasing your spending.

### Step 4: Configure Your Ad Group

After setting up your Campaign, configure the Ad Group.

This is where you define how and when your advertisements will be delivered.

**1. Choose Ad Placements**

Select where your ads can appear based on the placements available to your account.

If automatic placement is available and suitable for your objective, you can consider testing it first.

If you prefer more control, review the available placement options and choose those that fit your campaign.

**2. Select Target Locations**

Choose the countries or regions where you want your advertisements to appear.

For example:

- United States.
- United Kingdom.
- Japan.
- Canada.
- Australia.
- Other target markets.

For international e-commerce campaigns, select locations where you can actually deliver your products or provide your services.

**3. Configure Audience Targeting**

TikTok Ads provides different audience targeting options depending on your account and campaign objective.

These may include:

- Location.
- Age.
- Gender.
- Interests.
- User behaviors.
- Device-related options.

For example, if you are promoting fashion products, you can test audiences that match your product category and customer profile.

Avoid making your audience unnecessarily narrow at the beginning. Overly restrictive targeting can limit delivery and make it harder for the advertising system to find potential customers.

**4. Set Your Budget and Schedule**

Choose the budget and advertising schedule for your Ad Group.

You may be able to start immediately or select specific start and end dates.

For your first campaign, consider running a small test and reviewing the results before increasing the budget.

**5. Configure Bidding and Optimization**

Select the bidding and optimization settings available for your chosen objective.

Different objectives may offer different bidding strategies and optimization events.

If your goal is to generate website purchases, make sure your conversion tracking is correctly configured. Without reliable tracking, it may be difficult to measure the actual results of your advertising campaign.

### Step 5: Create Your Advertisement

After completing the Ad Group settings, move to the Ad level.

This is where you create the content that TikTok users will see.

**1. Select an Advertising Identity**

Choose the TikTok account or authorized identity that will represent your advertisement.

If you use an existing TikTok video, make sure you have the necessary rights and authorization to promote it.

**2. Upload Your Video or Images**

Upload the creative assets that match your selected ad format.

TikTok Ads supports different creative formats, and available options may vary by advertising product, placement, and account.

For many campaigns, vertical video is a practical format because it fits the mobile viewing experience.

Make sure your video is clear, engaging, and easy to understand.

**3. Write Your Advertising Copy**

Your advertising text should be concise and communicate the main benefit of your product or service.

For example:

- Discover a better way to shop online.
- Explore our latest product collection.
- Find the right solution for your business.
- Get started with our latest service.

Use natural language that matches your target audience.

Avoid misleading claims, exaggerated promises, or statements that cannot be supported.

**4. Add Your Destination URL**

If your objective is website traffic or website conversions, enter the correct landing page URL.

Before submitting your advertisement, open the link and check that:

- The page loads correctly.
- The content matches the advertisement.
- The mobile layout works properly.
- There are no broken links.
- Visitors can complete the intended action.

**5. Select a Call-to-Action Button**

Choose a suitable call-to-action option, such as Learn More, Shop Now, or Sign Up, depending on the options available in your account.

Make sure the button matches what users can actually do on your landing page.

### Step 6: Review and Submit Your Advertisement

Before submitting your campaign, review all the settings.

Pay particular attention to:

- Advertising objective.
- Target locations.
- Audience settings.
- Campaign and Ad Group budgets.
- Video and image quality.
- Advertising copy.
- Destination URL.
- Payment method.
- Compliance with TikTok advertising policies.

Once everything is correct, submit your advertisement for review.

Your ad can begin delivery after it passes the platform's review process and meets the campaign's delivery requirements.

Review times and approval decisions depend on TikTok's actual review process.

## 6. How to Set a TikTok Ads Budget

Budget management is one of the most important parts of advertising.

An unsuitable budget can make it difficult to collect enough data or may cause you to spend too much before understanding whether your campaign is working.

### 6.1 Start With a Small Test Budget

If you are new to TikTok Ads, avoid investing a large amount immediately.

Start with one clear advertising objective, a manageable audience, and several creative variations.

During the test, monitor the following metrics:

- Impressions.
- Video views.
- Clicks.
- Click-through rate (CTR).
- Cost per click (CPC).
- Website visits.
- Conversions.
- Cost per conversion.

These metrics help you understand whether your advertisement is attracting the right audience and producing useful results.

### 6.2 Avoid Changing Too Many Settings at Once

When a campaign first starts, the advertising system may need time to collect performance data.

Frequently changing the budget, audience, bidding strategy, and creative at the same time makes it difficult to identify what is affecting performance.

Make adjustments based on a clear testing plan rather than reacting to every short-term fluctuation.

### 6.3 Increase Your Budget Based on Results

If an Ad Group consistently generates valuable traffic or conversions, consider increasing its budget gradually.

If your advertisement receives impressions but few clicks, review the creative and advertising message.

If it receives clicks but few conversions, check your landing page, product pricing, checkout process, and audience relevance.

The goal is not simply to spend more money, but to improve the results generated by your advertising budget.

## 7. TikTok Ads Payment Methods: How to Pay for Advertising

You should monitor your advertising account's billing status regularly. Payment problems or insufficient balances can interrupt ad delivery.

TikTok Ads offers different billing options depending on your account's location, currency, eligibility, and setup.

### 7.1 Manual Payment

With Manual Payment, you add funds to your advertising account before running ads. Advertising charges are deducted from the available balance as your campaigns run.

If your balance runs out, your ads may stop delivering.

The general process is:

1. Log in to TikTok Ads Manager.
2. Open the **Tools** tab.
3. Under **Settings**, select **Billing**.
4. Open the **Payment** page.
5. Click **Add Balance**.
6. Enter the amount you want to add.
7. Select an available payment method.
8. Follow the instructions to complete the transaction.

After a successful payment, check your account balance to confirm that the funds have been added.

If your account is managed through Business Center, balance management may need to be completed through its Payment Management section.

### 7.2 Automatic Payment

With Automatic Payment, TikTok charges your selected payment method when your account reaches its billing threshold or billing date, whichever comes first.

To use this option, you need a valid payment method.

Important: TikTok states that prepaid cards cannot be used for automatic payments.

When adding a card, TikTok may perform a verification transaction. The verification amount and refund timing depend on the platform's current payment process.

Make sure your payment method remains valid and has sufficient available funds or credit to avoid interruptions.

### 7.3 Monthly Invoicing

Monthly Invoicing is a billing option available to eligible business advertisers.

It allows qualifying advertisers to receive advertising charges through a monthly invoicing arrangement.

Availability depends on TikTok's eligibility requirements and account approval. It is not necessarily available to every advertiser.

### 7.4 How to Check Your Available Payment Methods

To check the payment options available to your account:

1. Log in to TikTok Ads Manager.
2. Open **Tools**.
3. Under **Settings**, select **Billing**.
4. Go to the **Payment** page.
5. Click **Add Payment Method**.
6. Review the payment methods displayed for your account.

If your account is managed through Business Center, payment settings may be located under **Finance** and **Payment Management**.

Payment options can differ between countries and accounts, so always check your own advertising dashboard.

## 9. How to Optimize TikTok Ads Performance

Launching an advertisement is only the beginning.

To improve campaign performance, monitor your advertising data and make adjustments to your creative content, audience, and landing page.

### 9.1 Improve the First Few Seconds of Your Video

TikTok users scroll through content quickly, so the beginning of your video is especially important.

Consider these practices:

- Show the product or main message early.
- Avoid lengthy introductions.
- Use clear and engaging visuals.
- Explain the main benefit quickly.
- Create language versions for your target markets.

Your goal is to give viewers a reason to keep watching.

### 9.2 Test Different Advertising Creatives

Do not rely on a single video.

Prepare different creative variations, such as:

- Product demonstrations.
- Product tutorials.
- Real-world use cases.
- Product comparisons.
- Answers to common customer questions.

Compare the results to identify which content attracts the most relevant audience.

### 9.3 Analyze Your Click-Through Rate

If your advertisement receives many impressions but few clicks, review the following:

- Is the video engaging?
- Is the product's main benefit clear?
- Is the advertising copy relevant?
- Is the call-to-action easy to understand?
- Does the audience match the product?

Testing different creative approaches can help you identify what works best.

### 9.4 Review Website Conversion Performance

If your advertisement receives clicks but does not generate purchases or registrations, the problem may be on your website.

Check:

- Website loading speed.
- Product pricing.
- Product descriptions.
- Available payment methods.
- Registration and checkout steps.
- Mobile user experience.

Advertising performance should be evaluated together with website performance. A high number of clicks does not automatically mean a successful campaign.

### 9.5 Set Up Conversion Tracking

If your objective is to generate website conversions, consider setting up TikTok Pixel or other supported event tracking tools.

Proper event tracking can help you understand user actions such as:

- Page views.
- Add-to-cart events.
- Form submissions.
- Registrations.
- Purchases.

Make sure your tracking code is installed correctly and test the relevant events before relying on the data for campaign optimization.

## 10. Common TikTok Ads Questions (FAQ)

### 10.1 Can Individuals Run TikTok Ads?

Advertising availability depends on the advertiser's country or region, account type, and TikTok's current registration requirements.

Some locations or business categories may require additional business information or verification.

### 10.2 Do I Need a Business License to Run TikTok Ads?

Not necessarily in every situation.

Requirements vary by country, account type, and industry. Follow the registration and verification instructions displayed for your specific account.

### 10.3 What Is the Minimum Budget for TikTok Ads?

Minimum budget requirements may vary depending on the account's location, currency, campaign settings, and advertising level.

Check the minimum amount displayed in Ads Manager when creating your campaign.

### 10.4 How Long Does TikTok Ads Review Take?

Review times depend on the advertisement, account status, industry, and TikTok's review workload.

You can check the review status in Ads Manager. If an ad is rejected, review the reason provided by TikTok and make the necessary changes before submitting it again.

### 10.5 Why Does My TikTok Ad Get Impressions but No Clicks?

Possible reasons include:

- Unattractive video content.
- Unclear advertising copy.
- Weak product messaging.
- Poor audience targeting.
- An unsuitable ad placement.

Try testing different creative assets and reviewing your audience settings.

### 10.6 Can I Use a Virtual Credit Card for TikTok Ads?

It depends on the card type, account location, billing option, and TikTok's payment verification requirements.

TikTok specifically states that prepaid cards cannot be used for Automatic Payment. Always check the requirements for your own account before adding a payment method.

### 10.7 Why Has My TikTok Ads Payment Not Appeared in My Balance?

Possible reasons include payment processing delays, verification issues, declined transactions, or other billing problems.

Check your payment history and account balance first. If the transaction remains unresolved, contact your payment provider or TikTok support.

### 10.8 Can I Pause a TikTok Ad at Any Time?

Advertisers can generally manage campaign delivery through Ads Manager, including pausing or adjusting campaigns when needed.

The available actions may depend on account permissions and the current status of the advertisement.

### 10.9 Which TikTok Ads Objective Should Beginners Choose?

Start with your actual business goal.

If you want more website visitors, consider a Traffic objective. If you want purchases or registrations, select an available conversion or sales objective and make sure your website tracking is properly configured.
