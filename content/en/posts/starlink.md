# How Much Does Starlink Cost in 2027?

Starlink has become a popular satellite internet service for people who need reliable internet access in remote areas, rural communities, and locations where traditional broadband is limited.

Before subscribing, many people want to know: **How much does Starlink cost per month? How much does the equipment cost? Which subscription plan offers the best value?**

In this guide, we'll explain Starlink's monthly subscription fees, hardware costs, available plans, and other expenses to consider before placing an order in 2027.

Keep in mind that Starlink pricing varies by country and region. Equipment prices, subscription plans, taxes, and promotional offers may also change. Always check the official Starlink website for the latest price in your service area.

## 1. How Much Does Starlink Cost Per Month?

Starlink offers different subscription plans depending on how and where you use the service.

For most individual users, the main options to consider are Residential and Roam. Businesses may also be interested in Priority services.

The following are example U.S. prices currently listed for selected plans. They are reference prices, not guaranteed prices for every location or a confirmed global 2027 price list.

### Residential Plans

Residential plans are designed for home internet use at a fixed service address.

Example U.S. plan prices include:

- Residential 100 Mbps: $55 per month
- Residential 200 Mbps: $85 per month
- Residential Max: $130 per month

These plans are intended for everyday activities such as:

- Browsing websites
- Streaming movies and videos
- Online gaming
- Video conferencing
- Working or studying from home

The available speed tiers depend on your location. Not every plan is offered in every market.

If you mainly use the internet for everyday household activities, a lower-cost Residential plan may be sufficient. Households with multiple users or heavier internet demands may prefer a higher-tier option.

### Starlink Roam Plans

Roam plans are designed for people who need internet access while traveling or using Starlink in different locations.

Example U.S. prices include:

- Roam 100GB: $55 per month
- Roam 300GB: $80 per month
- Roam Unlimited: $175 per month

The data allowance and usage conditions vary by plan. Some limited-data plans provide a certain amount of high-speed data, followed by unlimited lower-speed data.

Roam may be suitable for:

- RV owners
- Campers
- Travelers
- Digital nomads
- People working in multiple locations

Before choosing a Roam plan, check its data limits, supported countries, and international usage policies.

### Starlink Priority Plans

Priority services are primarily intended for business customers and users who require additional network capabilities.

Depending on the service and market, these plans may offer different data allowances, network priority, and business-oriented features.

They are generally more suitable for commercial operations than ordinary household internet use.

## 2. How Much Does Starlink Equipment Cost?

In addition to the monthly subscription, new customers may need to purchase Starlink hardware.

The equipment price depends on the model, country, availability, and current promotions.

### Starlink Standard

Starlink Standard is commonly used for residential and fixed-location internet access.

A typical equipment package may include:

- Starlink satellite dish
- Wi-Fi router
- Power supply and cables
- Basic mounting accessories

It is suitable for homes, offices, and other locations where a fixed internet connection is needed.

Before purchasing, check the product description to confirm exactly which accessories are included.

### Starlink Mini

Starlink Mini is a more compact and portable option designed for users who need internet access while traveling.

Its features include:

- Compact and lightweight design
- Built-in Wi-Fi
- Portable setup
- DC power input
- Convenient use for outdoor activities

It may be a good choice for camping, road trips, and mobile work.

However, check local plan compatibility and usage restrictions before purchasing.

### How Much Should You Budget for Equipment?

Starlink does not have one worldwide equipment price.

For example, selected Starlink Roam hardware listings in Japan have shown prices starting from around ¥34,800. Actual prices may vary depending on the model, promotions, and order conditions.

Some markets may also offer equipment discounts, rental options, or other promotions.

Before ordering, check:

- Hardware purchase price
- Shipping fees
- Local taxes
- Installation costs
- Available rental or payment options

## 3. What Is the Total Cost of Starlink?

When calculating the cost of Starlink, don't look only at the monthly subscription fee.

There are several possible expenses to consider.

### Initial Equipment Cost

This is usually the main upfront expense for new customers.

If your market requires you to purchase the hardware, include the equipment price in your initial budget.

If equipment rental or promotional pricing is available, compare those options before ordering.

### Monthly Subscription Fee

The subscription fee is the main recurring expense.

For example, if your selected plan costs $55 per month, the basic subscription cost over 12 months would be:

$55 × 12 = $660 per year.

This calculation does not include hardware, taxes, shipping, or other additional charges.

### Other Possible Costs

Depending on your location and selected service, you may also need to pay for:

- Taxes
- Shipping
- Professional installation
- Additional data or services
- Plan upgrades
- Other optional accessories

Always review the final checkout amount before confirming your order.

## 4. Which Starlink Plan Should You Choose?

The right plan depends on your internet usage and where you intend to use the service.

### For Home Internet

Residential is generally the first option to consider if you need internet access at a fixed home address.

It is designed for everyday browsing, streaming, video calls, and remote work.

### For Travel and Outdoor Use

Roam may be more suitable if you frequently travel, use an RV, go camping, or work from different locations.

Check the data allowance and roaming conditions carefully before subscribing.

### For Business Use

Businesses that depend on internet connectivity may want to explore Priority services.

Consider network requirements, data allowances, equipment compatibility, and service terms before choosing a business plan.

### For Budget-Conscious Users

Compare the entry-level plans available in your area.

Also check equipment promotions and any additional fees. A low monthly price does not necessarily mean the lowest total cost.

## 6. What to Do If Your Starlink Payment Fails

If your Starlink payment is declined, try the following steps.

### Check Your Card Balance

Make sure your card has enough available funds to cover the subscription and any currency conversion or international transaction fees.

### Verify Your Billing Address

Check that your billing name, address, and postal code match the information registered with your card issuer.

### Enable International Online Payments

Some cards have restrictions on international transactions. Contact your card issuer to confirm whether overseas online payments are enabled.

### Check Recurring Payment Support

A payment method may support one-time transactions but reject recurring subscription charges.

Confirm that your card supports automatic recurring payments.

### Contact Starlink Support

If the payment still fails, sign in to your Starlink account and contact official support for assistance.

## 8. Is Starlink Worth the Cost?

Whether Starlink is worth the money depends on your location and internet requirements.

It may be a valuable option if you live in a rural area, work in remote locations, or cannot access reliable traditional broadband.

However, if you already have affordable fiber internet with good performance, compare the total cost before switching.

Consider the following factors:

- Is Starlink available at your address?
- Is the monthly subscription affordable?
- Can you accept the upfront equipment cost?
- Is your installation location free from major obstructions?
- Do you need internet access while traveling?
- Are cheaper broadband alternatives available?

Starlink is not automatically faster or cheaper than traditional broadband. Actual performance can depend on weather, satellite coverage, network congestion, and equipment placement.
