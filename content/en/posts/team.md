- Check whether international payments are enabled.
- Confirm that online transactions are permitted.

### 6.2 Insufficient Card Balance

Claude Team charges depend on the number and type of seats you purchase.

### 6.3 Incorrect Billing Information

Incorrect billing details may cause a transaction to be rejected.

### 6.4 Your Virtual Card Is Not Supported

Some virtual cards work for ordinary online purchases but may not support AI subscriptions or recurring payments.

Before trying again, ask your card provider:

- Are Anthropic transactions supported?
- Are recurring payments allowed?
- Are there merchant category restrictions?
- Are there transaction or monthly spending limits?

If a payment fails, avoid repeatedly submitting the same transaction without understanding the reason.

### 6.5 Account or Regional Restrictions

Your account region, payment card, and billing information may affect payment eligibility.

Use accurate account and billing details and follow the service's regional requirements.

If your payment method appears valid but the transaction continues to fail, contact Claude Support for assistance.

Claude Team and Claude Pro are designed for different types of users.

### Claude Pro Is Better for Individual Users

Claude Pro may be a better choice if you mainly use Claude for:

- Everyday AI conversations.
- Writing and translation.
- Research and analysis.
- File processing.
- Coding assistance.
- Personal productivity.

It is simpler to manage because you do not need to create an organization or manage multiple seats.

### Claude Team Is Better for Organizations

Claude Team is more suitable if several people in your company or team need access to Claude.

Its main advantages include:

- Centralized billing.
- Team member management.
- Different seat types.
- Organization-level administration.
- Shared workspace and collaboration features.

### Do Not Treat Team as a Shared Login Account

Claude Team is designed for individual members to access an organization through their own accounts.

It should not be treated as a way for several people to share one username and password.

Using individual member accounts makes it easier to manage permissions, access, and team changes.
