**MARU may accept certain virtual cards, but acceptance cannot be confirmed without checking the platform's actual payment options and policies.**

A virtual card provides digital payment details, such as a card number, expiration date, and security code. Depending on the issuer, it may support online purchases without requiring a physical card.

If MARU offers credit or debit card payments, you can check whether your virtual card is compatible with the available payment network and transaction requirements.

However, displaying a Visa or Mastercard logo does not mean that every virtual card will be accepted.

Before making a payment, check the following:

- **Accepted payment methods:** Does MARU offer a credit or debit card option at checkout?
- **Card compatibility:** Does your virtual card support the required transaction type?
- **International payments:** Are international online transactions enabled?
- **Available balance:** Is there enough money to cover the purchase and any applicable fees?
- **Billing information:** Does the billing address match the information associated with your card?
- **Merchant restrictions:** Does your card issuer allow payments to this type of merchant?

If MARU does not offer card payments or explicitly excludes your card type, you will need to use another payment method supported by the platform.

## 2. How to Pay for MARU with a Virtual Card

### Step 1: Check MARU's Payment Options

Sign in to your MARU account and open the relevant checkout, purchase, or subscription page.

Enter your card details on MARU's official payment page and follow the instructions.

If additional authentication is required, complete it through the legitimate verification process provided by your bank or card issuer.

After submitting the payment, check whether:

1. The checkout page confirms successful payment.

3. You have received a payment confirmation.
4. The transaction appears in your card account as completed, pending, or declined.

If the payment status is unclear, avoid submitting another payment until you have checked the first transaction.

## 3. Why Is Your Virtual Card Declined on MARU?

Even if your virtual card works on other websites, a particular MARU transaction may fail for several reasons.

### 1. MARU Does Not Accept Your Card Type

Merchants may apply different acceptance rules to virtual cards, prepaid cards, and traditional credit cards.

**How to fix it:**

Review MARU's payment instructions or contact its support team to confirm whether your specific card type is accepted. If it is not supported, choose another available payment method.

### 2. Insufficient Available Balance

Your payment may be declined if the available balance does not cover the purchase and any applicable fees.

**How to fix it:**

Check your card balance, pending transactions, and spending limits. Make sure sufficient funds are available before trying again.

### 3. International Payments Are Restricted

Some virtual cards restrict international transactions or certain merchant categories.

**How to fix it:**

Contact your card issuer to confirm whether international online payments are enabled and whether any restrictions apply to MARU transactions.

### 4. Incorrect Billing Address

A mismatch between the billing details entered at checkout and the information associated with your card may cause the transaction to fail.

**How to fix it:**

Check your billing address, city, region, and postal code. For virtual cards, use the actual billing information provided by the issuer rather than entering an arbitrary address.

### 5. Incorrect CVV or Expiration Date

An incorrect card number, expiration date, or security code may prevent the payment from being processed.

**How to fix it:**

Review the card details carefully. If your card has been replaced or reissued, make sure you are using the latest information.

### 6. The Card Issuer Flags the Transaction

Unusual spending patterns, repeated payment attempts, or merchant restrictions may trigger security controls.

**How to fix it:**

Stop repeated attempts and check whether your issuer has placed a restriction on the transaction. Contact the issuer if you need clarification or additional authorization.

### 7. A Temporary Payment Processing Error Occurs

Sometimes, a payment fails because of a temporary technical problem rather than an issue with the card itself.

**How to fix it:**

Check your MARU account and card transaction history. If the payment was not completed, you may try again later. If the transaction is still pending, confirm its status before making another payment.

If the problem continues, contact MARU support and provide the exact error message.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Does MARU definitely support virtual cards?

This cannot be confirmed for every virtual card without checking MARU's current payment options and policies. Consult the official checkout page or contact MARU support for confirmation.

### 2. Will a Visa or Mastercard virtual card always work?

No. The card network logo does not guarantee acceptance by every merchant. Approval can depend on the issuer, transaction type, region, and merchant restrictions.

### 3. Why can I add my virtual card but not complete a payment?

Adding a card and authorizing a transaction are different processes. During payment, the system may also check your balance, spending limits, billing details, international payment permissions, and merchant restrictions.

### 4. Can I retry the payment immediately after it fails?

First, check whether the original transaction was declined or is still pending. If a pending authorization or charge appears in your account, confirm its status with your issuer before trying again to avoid duplicate payments.

### 5. What should I know before using a virtual card for a MARU subscription?

If MARU offers recurring subscriptions, confirm that your card supports recurring charges. Maintain sufficient funds for future renewals and review the subscription price, billing cycle, and cancellation policy.

### 6. What if my card is charged but MARU does not activate the service?

Check your order status and card transaction history. If the payment is confirmed but the service remains inactive, save the order number, transaction date, amount, and error message, then contact MARU through its official support channels.

Do not make another payment until the status of the original transaction has been clarified.
