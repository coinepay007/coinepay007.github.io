## **1. What Is Shopee?**

Shopee is an online shopping platform operated by Sea Limited. It serves multiple markets, particularly in Southeast Asia, and provides localized shopping experiences for different countries and regions.

Users can access Shopee through its mobile application or website to browse products, place orders, make payments, and track deliveries.

Some of the main features include:

- **Online Shopping:** Purchase clothing, electronics, household products, beauty items, and more.
- **Product Search:** Find products using keywords and categories.
- **Discounts and Vouchers:** Access promotions, coupons, and special offers.
- **Order Management:** Review purchases and check delivery updates.
- **Seller Services:** Eligible users can open online stores and sell products.

## **7. What Payment Methods Does Shopee Accept?**

Shopee payment options vary by country, marketplace, account status, and order conditions.

Common payment methods may include the following.

### **Credit and Debit Cards**

Some regional Shopee marketplaces support eligible credit and debit cards.

Whether a particular card works depends on the marketplace's payment rules, card network, issuing bank, and transaction verification requirements.

### **E-Wallets**

In certain markets, Shopee supports local electronic wallets.

Users can select an available wallet during checkout and follow the authorization instructions.

### **Cash on Delivery**

Some eligible orders and locations may support Cash on Delivery.

This option allows customers to pay when their packages arrive, subject to local availability and order requirements.

### **Other Local Payment Methods**

Depending on the country, Shopee may also provide bank transfers or other local payment services.

Always check the payment methods displayed on the checkout page rather than assuming that a payment option is available in every country.

### **What to Do If a Payment Fails**

If your payment does not go through, check the following:

1. Confirm that the selected payment method is supported.
2. Check your available balance or credit limit.
3. Make sure your card is enabled for the transaction.
4. Check whether your bank requires additional verification.
5. Review any error message displayed by Shopee.

If your bank shows a completed charge but Shopee does not confirm the order, avoid paying again immediately. Check the transaction status and contact the platform or your bank if necessary.

## **10. Common Shopee Registration and Shopping Problems**

New users may encounter several common issues when registering or shopping on Shopee.

### **Why Am I Not Receiving the Registration Verification Code?**

First, confirm that your phone number or email address is correct.

Then check your message filters, spam folder, and network connection.

If the problem continues, wait a while before requesting another verification code.

Account restrictions may be related to security checks, verification problems, or marketplace policies.

Follow the instructions provided by Shopee and use official support channels if you need further assistance.

Avoid using unofficial registration services or tools that claim to bypass account restrictions.

Shopee has different regional marketplaces, and account systems and login requirements may vary.

Whether the same account can be used across different markets depends on the applicable regional rules.

Check the login requirements of the marketplace you want to access.

### **Why Can't I Find a Product on Shopee?**

A product may be unavailable because it has been removed, sold out, or is not listed in your selected marketplace.

Try using different keywords or adjusting your search filters.

### **Why Is My Shopee Payment Declined?**

Possible reasons include insufficient funds, bank restrictions, incorrect card information, unsupported payment methods, or additional verification requirements.

Check the payment error message and contact your bank or Shopee support if necessary.

### **Can I Buy Products From Shopee Overseas?**

International shopping availability depends on the product, seller, marketplace, and shipping destination.

Not every seller offers international delivery, and some products may have geographical restrictions.

Always confirm shipping availability and additional costs before placing an order.
