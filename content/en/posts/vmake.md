# 1. Insufficient Card Balance

Insufficient balance is one of the most common reasons for payment failure.

Some users only prepare the exact amount shown on the subscription page, but the final payment may also include:

- Taxes
- Currency conversion
- International transaction fees
- Card issuer fees
- Payment processing fees

Therefore, even if the subscription costs a certain amount and your card has exactly the same balance, the transaction may still fail.

## Solution

It is recommended to:

- Check the available balance
- Keep some extra funds on the card
- Check for any pending or frozen amounts
- Confirm whether currency conversion fees apply

---

# 2. The Card Does Not Support International Payments

Vmake Labs is an international digital service platform.

If your bank card or virtual card only supports domestic transactions, the payment may be rejected when you try to subscribe.

Common error messages include:

**Card Declined**

or:

**Transaction Not Allowed**

## Solution

Check whether your card has:

**International Payments**

enabled.

Also confirm that overseas online merchant transactions are allowed.

If not, contact your card issuer or check the card settings.

---

# 3. Online Payments Are Disabled

Some cards support international transactions but have online payments disabled by default.

In this situation, the card may work in physical stores but fail when paying for services such as Vmake.

Check whether the following feature is enabled:

**Online Payments**

or:

**E-commerce Transactions**

---

# 4. Recurring Payments Are Not Supported

Vmake Labs subscription services may involve automatic renewal.

This means the payment method may need to support:

**Recurring Payments**

If the card only supports one-time transactions, you may experience:

- Initial subscription failure
- First payment succeeds but renewal fails
- Automatic renewal is declined

## Solution

Ask your card issuer whether the card supports:

- Subscription Payments
- Recurring Transactions
- Automatic Renewal

---

# 5. Incorrect CVV

If the payment page asks for:

**CVV / CVC / Security Code**

you must enter the code exactly as provided by your bank or virtual card platform.

Common mistakes include:

- Entering the wrong CVV
- Using an old CVV
- CVV changed after the card was regenerated
- Adding unnecessary spaces

Possible error messages include:

**Invalid CVV**

**Security Code Incorrect**

or similar warnings.

## Solution

Open your bank or virtual card platform and verify:

- Card Number
- Expiration Date
- CVV

Then enter the information again.

---

# 6. Incorrect Expiration Date

Payment pages usually require:

**Expiration Date**

The format is generally:

**MM / YY**

If the month or year is entered incorrectly, the authorization may fail even when the card number and CVV are correct.

Always use the exact information shown in your card dashboard.

---

# 7. Billing Address Does Not Match

Some online payment systems may verify billing information.

This may include:

- Name
- Address
- City
- State / Province
- Postal Code
- Country

If the information differs significantly from what the card issuer expects, the payment may fail.

## Solution

Use:

**Billing information accepted by the card issuer**

instead of entering random or fabricated addresses.

---

# 8. Too Many Payment Attempts in a Short Time

When one payment fails, some users repeatedly click the payment button.

For example:

First attempt fails → try again → try again → submit multiple times.

This behavior may trigger:

- Bank risk controls
- Card issuer security systems
- Payment gateway fraud protection
- Merchant anti-fraud systems

Eventually, the card may show:

**Card Declined**

even if it was originally usable.

## Solution

If the first payment fails:

1. Check the error message
2. Check the balance
3. Verify the CVV
4. Check international payment permissions
5. Confirm billing information
6. Identify the likely cause before trying again

Avoid repeated payment attempts within a short period.

---

# 9. The Card Issuer Rejects the Transaction

Sometimes all card details are correct, but the bank still declines the transaction.

Possible reasons include:

- Overseas transaction risk controls
- Unusual merchant detection
- First international transaction on a new card
- Unusual transaction amount
- Subscription payment restrictions
- Merchant category restrictions

In this situation, Vmake may not be able to resolve the issue directly.

## Solution

Contact the card issuer and ask:

**Why was this Vmake Labs online transaction declined?**

Confirm whether you need to:

- Enable international payments
- Remove transaction restrictions
- Enable subscription payments
- Allow the merchant category

---

Some virtual cards may restrict:

- The card has sufficient balance
- The CVV is correct
- The expiration date is correct

# 11. App Store or Google Play Subscription Issues

Vmake Labs subscriptions may also be purchased through mobile app stores such as the Apple App Store or Google Play.

A subscription purchased through one app store may not always transfer directly to another platform or store version.

For example:

You subscribe through:

**Apple App Store**

Then you download the app through another channel.

The purchased benefits may not automatically appear in the other version.

If a mobile subscription payment fails, check:

- Apple ID payment method
- Google Play payment method
- App Store region
- Google Play region
- Store account balance

---

# 12. Insufficient Balance During Automatic Renewal

Automatic subscriptions may charge the payment method again at the end of the current billing cycle.

If the account or card does not have enough balance, the renewal can fail.

This means:

**A successful first subscription does not guarantee that future renewals will succeed.**

Before the renewal date:

- Make sure the card has not expired
- Keep enough balance
- Do not freeze the card
- Keep recurring payments enabled
- Check the next billing date

---

# 13. Payment Succeeded but Membership Is Not Active

Sometimes users may see a successful charge, but the membership status in Vmake has not updated.

In this situation, try:

1. Refreshing the Vmake page
2. Logging out and back in
3. Checking the account used for the purchase
4. Checking the App Store or Google Play account
5. Reviewing the order or payment record

If the issue remains, contact official support.

Vmake Labs support email:

**[support@vmake.ai](mailto:support@vmake.ai)**

# 14. How to Cancel Vmake Automatic Renewal

If you no longer want the subscription, cancel automatic renewal through the platform used to purchase it.

### iOS

Go to:

**Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions**

Find Vmake Labs and manage the subscription.

### Android

Go to:

**Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**

Find Vmake Labs and manage the subscription.

Simply deleting the app may not cancel an active subscription.

Therefore, if you stop using the service, always check the subscription status separately.
