This is one of the most common reasons for overseas payment failures.

Some bank cards restrict:

- International transactions
- Foreign currency payments
- Overseas online purchases

Symptoms:

Your card works locally but fails on Anthropic.

### Solution:

Check your card settings:

✅ Enable international payments  
✅ Enable online transactions  
✅ Allow foreign currency transactions
