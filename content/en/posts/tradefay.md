## 1. Why Is My TradeDay Payment Failing?

### 1. Insufficient Available Balance

One of the most common reasons for a failed payment is an insufficient available balance.

Your account balance may differ from the amount you can actually spend. Some funds may be temporarily held because of pending transactions, and additional charges may apply depending on your payment method.

For example:

- Purchase amount: $100
- Available balance: $80
- Result: The payment may be declined.

Currency conversion fees and other applicable charges may also affect the final amount.

**How to fix it:**

1. Check your card's available balance.
2. Make sure you have enough funds to cover the full payment.
3. Check for pending transactions or temporarily held funds.
4. Confirm that your card's spending limits have not been exceeded.
5. Retry the payment after confirming that sufficient funds are available.

### 2. Credit or Debit Card Declined

If you receive a **Card Declined** message, your bank or card issuer may have rejected the transaction.

Possible reasons include:

- International online payments are restricted.
- Your bank blocks certain merchant categories.
- Your card's spending limit has been reached.
- The transaction triggered a fraud prevention check.
- Your card has been temporarily frozen.
- Additional verification is required.

A card may work on other websites but still be declined for a specific transaction.

**How to fix it:**

Contact your card issuer and ask whether it received the TradeDay payment authorization request.

If the transaction was declined, ask for the reason and whether your card supports the payment type involved. Complete any required security verification through your bank's official channels.

Avoid submitting repeated payment attempts before identifying the cause.

### 3. Incorrect Card Details

Incorrect payment information can prevent TradeDay from processing your transaction.

Common mistakes include:

- An incorrect card number.
- An expired card.
- An incorrect CVV or CVC security code.
- Outdated card details saved in your browser.
- Incorrect billing information.

**How to fix it:**

Carefully review the information entered on the payment page.

If your card has recently been renewed or replaced, use the updated details. If your browser automatically fills in outdated information, remove it and enter the correct details manually.

Never share your full card number, CVV, password, or one-time verification code through ordinary support messages.

### 4. Billing Address Mismatch

Some payment processors verify billing information before authorizing a transaction. A mismatch may cause the payment to fail.

Check the following details:

- Cardholder name.
- Billing address.
- City and region.
- Postal or ZIP code.
- Country or region.

**How to fix it:**

Enter accurate billing information according to the payment form's requirements.

If you are using a virtual card, check with the card issuer for the correct billing information associated with that card.

Do not use a false address to bypass payment verification. This may cause further declines or security reviews.

### 5. International Payment Restrictions

Some cards support ordinary online purchases but have restrictions on international transactions or particular types of payments.

Possible causes include:

- International transactions are disabled.
- Certain merchant categories are blocked.
- The card issuer restricts specific transaction types.
- Additional authorization is required.
- The payment processor cannot complete the authorization.

**How to fix it:**

Contact your bank or card issuer to confirm whether the transaction is permitted.

If your current card cannot support the required payment type, check which alternative payment methods are available on the TradeDay checkout page.

### 6. 3D Secure or Security Verification Failure

Some card transactions require additional authentication, such as 3D Secure (3DS), depending on the issuer and payment process.

A payment may fail if the verification page does not load or the required authentication is not completed.

**How to fix it:**

1. Check whether your bank requires additional authentication.
2. Make sure you can receive verification codes or approval notifications.
3. Follow your bank's instructions to complete authentication.
4. Try another supported browser if the verification page does not load.
5. Contact your bank or TradeDay support if the issue continues.

### 7. Temporary Payment Processing Problems

Not every failed payment is caused by the cardholder. A transaction may fail because of a temporary processing issue or an authorization problem between the payment provider and the card issuer.

**How to fix it:**

- Check your TradeDay account for the current order or payment status.
- Confirm whether your bank shows a pending charge.
- Avoid paying again while the original transaction's status is unclear.
- Record the error message and approximate transaction time.
- Contact TradeDay support if the problem persists.

## 3. How to Fix TradeDay Subscription Payment Failures

If you have already purchased a TradeDay service but your next payment fails, check your subscription status promptly. A failed payment may affect access to your account or services, depending on the applicable billing rules.

### Step 1: Check Your Subscription Status

Log in to your TradeDay account and review the available billing, subscription, or account information.

Check whether:

- Your subscription is still active.
- A renewal payment is pending.
- Your account displays a payment error.
- You need to update your payment method.

If your card is about to expire, updating your payment details in advance may help prevent renewal problems.

### Step 2: Review Your Payment Method

Make sure the card or other payment method you use is still valid and supports the required transaction.

If the card has expired or been replaced, update the details wherever TradeDay allows you to manage your payment information.

### Step 3: Check the Status of the Previous Transaction

Before submitting another payment, confirm whether the previous attempt was declined, is still pending, or was successfully charged.

This is particularly important if your bank shows a pending transaction but TradeDay displays an error.

### Step 4: Contact TradeDay Support

If your subscription remains inactive or the renewal continues to fail, contact TradeDay at [support@tradeday.com](mailto:support@tradeday.com).

Provide the approximate payment time, purchase amount, error message, and relevant order information. Ask the support team to confirm your subscription status and explain the available recovery options.

Do not assume that a failed payment will automatically be retried or that your account will remain active. The outcome may depend on the payment method and the terms applicable to your purchase.

## 4. TradeDay Charged Me, but My Account Is Not Activated

Sometimes your bank may show a payment while the corresponding TradeDay service remains inactive.

Do not immediately submit another payment. First, determine whether the original transaction was completed or is still being processed.

### What Should You Check?

**Step 1: Review your bank statement.**

Determine whether the transaction is completed or appears only as a pending authorization.

**Step 2: Check your TradeDay account.**

Review your order status, subscription information, and any payment notifications.

**Step 3: Keep the transaction details.**

Record the transaction date, amount, order information, and payment status shown by your bank.

**Step 4: Contact TradeDay support.**

If your bank confirms that the payment was completed but the service remains inactive, contact [support@tradeday.com](mailto:support@tradeday.com) and request a payment review.

For security, never send your full card number, CVV, password, or one-time verification codes in ordinary email messages.

## 5. Can You Get a Refund for a Failed TradeDay Payment?

A declined payment, a duplicate charge, and a completed subscription payment are different situations and should be handled separately.

TradeDay's published terms state that subscription fees are generally non-refundable except where required by law. Review the terms that apply to your purchase before submitting a refund request.

Consider the following situations:

- **Payment declined without a completed charge:** Check whether the bank shows only a temporary authorization.
- **Payment completed but service not activated:** Ask TradeDay support to investigate the order.
- **Duplicate charge:** Keep records of both transactions and request a review.
- **Unexpected subscription renewal:** Review the applicable cancellation and refund terms.
- **Payment failure followed by account restrictions:** Ask support to confirm your account status and recovery options.

Do not assume that you qualify for a refund simply because you no longer use the service. Eligibility depends on the applicable terms, transaction status, and any relevant legal requirements.

If you believe a charge is incorrect, contact TradeDay and your payment provider to understand the available dispute process.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions About TradeDay Payment Failures

### 1. Why does TradeDay keep saying Payment Failed?

Possible causes include insufficient funds, incorrect card details, international payment restrictions, billing address mismatches, or payment processing issues.

Start by checking your error message and asking your bank whether it received and declined the transaction.

### 2. What payment methods does TradeDay accept?

TradeDay's available payment options may depend on the purchase page and the service you select. Its current checkout page is the best place to confirm which methods are available for your specific order.

Do not assume that every card type or digital wallet is supported for every purchase.

### 3. Why does my card work on other websites but fail on TradeDay?

Different merchants and payment processors may apply different card acceptance rules, security checks, and transaction requirements.

A card that works elsewhere may still be declined for a specific TradeDay transaction.

### 4. Will TradeDay automatically retry a failed payment?

You should not assume that every failed payment will be retried automatically. The process may depend on the payment method and the terms applicable to your subscription.

Check your account notifications and contact support if the renewal fails or your account status changes.

### 5. Can a failed payment affect my trading evaluation account?

It may affect access to your purchased services, depending on your account status and the applicable terms.

If you have already started an evaluation, contact TradeDay support promptly to confirm whether your account remains active and what steps are available to resolve the billing issue.

### 6. What information should I provide to TradeDay support?

Prepare the following information:

- The email address associated with your account.
- The approximate payment date and time.
- The purchase amount.
- The exact error message.
- The transaction status shown by your bank.
- The order or transaction reference, if available.

Never provide your full card number, CVV, account password, or one-time verification code.

### 7. Should I try another card immediately?

Not necessarily. First, confirm that the previous transaction was declined and is not still pending.

If the original payment is confirmed as unsuccessful, you can check whether another payment method accepted by TradeDay is available.
