## 2. Card Does Not Support Recurring Payments

❌ Automatic renewal  
❌ Recurring charges

International payment systems may verify:

- Name

- Postal code
- Country

- Wrong billing address
- Incorrect postal code

## 4. Bank Security Block

- First international purchase

- USD transaction
- Unusual activity

- Check bank notifications
- Contact your bank
- Try again later

- Subscription price
- Currency conversion differences
- Possible fees
