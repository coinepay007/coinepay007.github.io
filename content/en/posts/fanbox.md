# Why Did My Fanbox Payment Fail?

## 1. Insufficient Balance

The most common reason is not having enough available balance.

For example:

**Card Balance: $10**

**Fanbox Payment: $8**

Even if the balance appears sufficient, currency conversion, authorization holds, or other fees may affect the available amount.

Make sure:

**Available Balance > Payment Amount**

---

## 2. International Payments Are Disabled

Fanbox payments may involve international online transactions.

Some cards have international payments disabled by default.

Check whether your card supports:

**International Payments**

**Online Payments**

**Cross-Border Transactions**

If international payments are restricted, the transaction may be declined by your card issuer.

---

## 3. Incorrect Card Details

Check the following information carefully:

**Card Number**

**Expiration Date**

**CVV / CVC**

A single incorrect digit can cause:

**Payment Failed**

or:

**Card Declined**

If you use a virtual card, check the latest card details directly from your card provider.

---

## 4. Incorrect CVV / CVC

An incorrect CVV or CVC can cause the payment to be rejected.

This is especially important if your virtual card has been:

**Reissued**

**Replaced**

or:

**Regenerated**

The security code may have changed.

Always use the current CVV / CVC shown in your virtual card account.

---

## 5. Expired Card

Check the card's:

**Expiration Date**

If the card has expired, Fanbox cannot complete the payment.

For subscriptions, also remember to update your payment method before the card expires.

---

## 6. Payment Limit Is Too Low

Your card may have enough balance but still fail because of a transaction limit.

For example:

**Available Balance: $100**

**Fanbox Payment: $20**

**Single Transaction Limit: $10**

The payment can still be declined.

Check:

**Single Transaction Limit**

**Daily Payment Limit**

**Online Payment Limit**

**International Payment Limit**

---

## 7. Bank Risk Controls

Banks and card issuers may decline transactions that appear unusual or risky.

Possible triggers include:

**International Transactions**

**New Merchants**

**First-Time Payments**

**Multiple Failed Attempts**

**Unusual Transaction Patterns**

If your card information and balance are correct but the payment continues to fail, contact your card issuer to check whether the transaction was blocked.

---

## 8. Billing Information Does Not Match

Some payment systems may check billing information.

If Fanbox asks for billing details, make sure the information is accurate, such as:

**Name**

**Billing Address**

**City**

**State**

**ZIP Code**

**Country**

Avoid using random or fake billing information, as mismatched details can increase the chance of payment verification failure.

---

## 9. Virtual Card Does Not Support the Payment

Not every virtual card has the same features.

Some cards may have restrictions on:

**Online Payments**

**International Payments**

**Recurring Payments**

**Merchant Categories**

Therefore, a virtual card that works on one website may not necessarily work on Fanbox.

Before using a virtual card, check its supported payment types.

# How to Fix Fanbox Payment Failed

If your Fanbox payment fails, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Check Card Status

Make sure:

**Card Status = Active**

The card should not be:

**Frozen**

**Locked**

**Suspended**

or:

**Expired**

---

### Step 2: Check Available Balance

Make sure you have enough available balance to cover the payment.

It is better to keep some extra balance for possible:

**Currency Conversion**

**Authorization Holds**

or other charges.

---

### Step 3: Check Card Information

Verify:

**Card Number**

**Expiration Date**

**CVV / CVC**

Make sure you are using the latest card information.

---

### Step 4: Enable Online Payments

Confirm that:

**Online Payments = Enabled**

If online payments are disabled, the card may be declined.

---

### Step 5: Enable International Payments

If the transaction is treated as an international payment, make sure:

**International Payments = Enabled**

---

### Step 6: Check Your Payment Limit

Make sure your transaction limit is higher than the Fanbox subscription amount.

---

### Step 7: Check Billing Information

Enter accurate billing information that matches the card or virtual card account requirements.

---

### Step 8: Try Again Once

After fixing the problem, try the payment again.

Avoid repeatedly submitting the same failed payment:

**Pay → Failed → Pay → Failed → Pay → Failed**

Multiple failed attempts in a short period may trigger additional risk controls.
